What Is cbcSchool App?

cbcSchool App is a free school management system built specifically for Kenyan schools running the Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC/CBE). Teachers mark attendance on their Android phones — even offline — and results sync to the cloud automatically. Headteachers get a full dashboard; parents get a portal with M-Pesa fee payment and WhatsApp alerts.

Key Features

  • Digital attendance register — works on 2G, 3G or no network
  • CBC report cards with EE/ME/AE/BE levels, downloadable as PDF
  • M-Pesa STK Push fee collection with auto ledger & receipts
  • WhatsApp parent alerts for absence, results and broadcasts
  • KICD strand/sub-strand curriculum auto-seeded per school
  • Teacher, Parent and Headteacher dashboards (three portals, one system)
  • Parent School Marketplace for premium subscribers
