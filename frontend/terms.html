2. Description of Service

cbcSchool App is a free, offline-first Progressive Web App (PWA) for Kenyan schools providing: digital attendance tracking, CBC report card generation, M-Pesa fee collection via STK Push, WhatsApp parent notifications, and portals for teachers, parents, headteachers, and a school marketplace. The Service is provided "as is" and "as available."

3. Eligibility

  • Schools must be registered educational institutions in Kenya
  • Users must be at least 18 years old (teachers, headteachers, parents)
  • Parents paying premium fees must have a valid M-Pesa account

4. Accounts & Access

  • Schools register via a headteacher; a unique school ID is issued
  • Teachers and headteachers log in via phone OTP (no passwords)
  • Parents access the portal via phone OTP; no app install required
  • You are responsible for keeping your phone number secure
  • Unauthorised access attempts will result in account suspension

5. Pricing & Payments

  • School Plan: Free forever for schools, teachers, headteachers
  • Parent Premium: KSh 100 per term per child (optional)
  • Payments processed via M-Pesa STK Push (Safaricom Daraja API)
  • No refunds for partial terms; premium features activate on confirmed payment
  • Prices in KES; subject to change with 30 days' notice

6. Offline-First & Data Sync

  • Schools own their data; export available anytime (CSV/PDF)
  • We are not liable for data loss if device is lost before sync
